## About me

I work across **IT infrastructure, systems administration and cybersecurity**, with a strong focus on self-hosted environments and practical operations.

My work includes Linux and Windows administration, identity and access management, networking, DNS and mail infrastructure, monitoring, incident handling, troubleshooting and automation. I also develop and maintain projects under the **[iTELade](https://github.com/iTELade)** organization.
